# Results

This directory contains reproducible outputs from executed experiments. Primary bounded quick artifacts are under `a/` through `j/`; separate completed full-profile artifacts for A–F are under `a/full/` through `f/full/`. Cross-track validation is recorded in `artifact_validation.json` and `.csv`.

CSV/JSON files are the source of measured claims; SVG figures are derived from them. G preserves real QEMU console/build logs, while J preserves real pytest profiling and isolated mutation logs. G–J full profiles remain `NOT RUN` and have no full artifact directories.

Use the [coverage matrix](../docs/coverage_matrix.md), [final handoff](../docs/final_handoff.md), and individual [track READMEs](../tracks/) to navigate the evidence and reproduction commands.
